Privacy Policy for KidProof

Effective Date: November 25, 2026

Developer: Alaa Mohamed Bakhit

1. Overview

KidProof is a local privacy protection tool designed to protect your device and personal data from accidental tampering by children. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it.

2. Data Collection and Storage

KidProof does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personal data to external servers or third parties. All operations, including photo encryption and app locking, are performed 100% locally on your device without relying on cloud storage.

3. Permissions Used & Why

  • Camera: Used solely to block unauthorized camera access by other apps while a child is using the device. The app does not take photos or record videos.
  • Microphone (Record Audio): Used solely to restrict microphone access and prevent unintended audio recordings or calls. The app does not record or listen to your audio.
  • Query All Packages (Installed Apps List): Used to display your installed apps locally so you can choose which apps to lock or restrict.
  • Accessibility Service: Used strictly to detect when a restricted app is opened and automatically enforce your lock/protection rules. No screen content or personal data is read, collected, or shared through this service.

4. Data Subject Rights & Contact

Since no data is collected or stored on our servers, you maintain full control over your data on your device. Uninstalling the app removes all local app configurations. For any inquiries, contact us at: Alaaasd707@gmail.com
